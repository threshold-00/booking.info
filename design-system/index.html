Tokens

Colour, type, spacing, radius, border, shadow, motion and breakpoints. Everything below reads from /assets/tokens.css. No value here is invented; see the brief, section 4.

Colour

Ink, paper and greys only: there is no accent colour (green retired 22 Oct 2026). Hover is grey: --color-hover behind ink text on paper surfaces (pills, menus, light buttons on dark), --color-hover-dark on filled ink buttons and the map pin. Links go ink and underline on hover. Success and status are ink text with an icon (✓), never a colour. Hover is never the only focus cue: :focus-visible is always a 2px ink outline (paper on ink).

Components

Every interactive component in default, hover (pinned with .is-hover), focus-visible, active and disabled states, plus loading where relevant.

Mobile rules (22 Oct 2026). Tap targets are at least 44px: buttons are 44px tall, and controls that look smaller (chips, Grid and Map tabs, breadcrumb, footer and photo credit links, map zoom) get a ::before inset that makes up the 44px. Text is at least 12px for captions, credits, breadcrumbs, footer links and booking labels. Chivo Mono caps are for short labels only, under about six words: labels, pills, prices, buttons and the hero subtext. Anything that runs to more than one line is Neue Haas in sentence case. Selects keep 16px text, or iOS zooms in.

.filter-row (6.25): the Experiences filters sit on the card grid, four columns with the cards' 20px gutter. Type runs across the top, with its sub-chips under it; under that, Location is column 1 and Duration column 2. From 821 to 1100px Location and Duration take half each; on phones everything stacks. Controls are 32px tall with a 1px --line-ui border, --paper fill, 12px side padding and 12px text, and sub-chips are 28px. On phones chips are 36px and selects 40px with 16px text, so iOS doesn't zoom. Labels sit 8px above. Spacing: title row to Type 32px, Type to Location 24px, filters to the count line 40px, count line to cards 16px. Duration buckets: 60 minutes or less; 61 to 300 minutes; over 300 on one day; two days or more. Each experience's durationMinutes (and durationDays) lives in its data file, and tools/card-durations.mjs copies it onto the homepage cards.

FAQ

How does Threshold make money?

Free to join. From 1 May 2027, businesses pay a 12% fee, including GST and card fees, only on bookings made through Threshold. There's no subscription and no setup fee.

Is there live availability?

Not yet. You book through the business's own system; Threshold doesn't hold a live calendar.
