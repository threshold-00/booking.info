Colour, type, spacing, radius, border, shadow, layers, motion and breakpoints. Everything below reads from /assets/tokens.css, which mirrors the body.rd layer of the live styles.css. Update both together. Last synced to live getrealdays.com on 26 Oct 2026.

Colour

Ink, paper and greys only: there is no accent colour (green retired 22 Oct 2026). Hover is grey: --color-hover behind ink text on paper surfaces (chips, menus, light buttons on dark), --color-hover-dark on filled ink buttons and the map pin. Links go ink and underline on hover. Success and status are ink text with an icon (✓), never a colour. Hover is never the only focus cue: :focus-visible is always a 2px ink outline (paper on ink).

Chivo Mono is the only mono and it is always uppercase, weight 400.

Components

Every interactive component in default, hover (pinned with .is-hover), focus-visible, active and disabled states, plus loading where relevant. Mirrors the live body.rd layer; where this page and the live styles.css disagree, live wins.

Rules (22 and 23 Oct 2026). Buttons, chips and selects are 40px and square; a ::before inset makes up the 44px tap target. Text is at least 12px.
