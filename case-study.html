Salty Cowboy

Horse riding on rescued horses. Beach rides, sunset rides and lessons for all levels.

The business

Salty Cowboy is a horse rescue and riding centre in Bali, run by Simone. Every horse here has a story. Some were retired from work, others rescued from neglect. Now they carry riders along black sand beaches, through rice paddies at sunset, and around the paddock for anyone learning for the first time.

What you get

Real riding, not a tourist trot. Whether you've never been on a horse or you've ridden your whole life, Simone matches you to a horse that suits you. Beach rides start from the stables and head out along the sand. Sunset rides time it so you're watching the light drop over the water. Lessons start in the paddock and build from there.

The booking

Pick the ride you want, choose a date, and fill in the details. Simone will be in touch to confirm and answer any questions. Rides suit all experience levels, and if you have any concerns about weight limits or fitness, she'll help you find the right option.
