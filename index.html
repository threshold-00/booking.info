Frequently asked questions

What is RealDays?

RealDays is a booking site for small, owner-run experiences, run by the local people who started them. Every experience so far is in Victoria.

What are last-minute spots?

Spare places in a class or session in the next week, posted by the business, often below their usual price. Each spot shows the price now, the usual price and how many places are left.

How do I book a last-minute spot?

Tap the spot, add your name and email, and pay by card on Stripe’s secure page. The price is locked when you pay. Your confirmation email has the business’s mobile and email. Bookings close 2 hours before the start.

Can I cancel a last-minute spot?

Last-minute spots are final, so there’s no refund if you cancel. If the business cancels, you get a full refund. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law still apply.

How do I hear about spots first?

Join the last-minute list. We send one email a week, on Thursdays at midday, and only when there are spots. Unsubscribe any time.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You only need your name and email. Some experiences book on the business’s own website, and their Book button takes you there.

How is this different from Viator or GetYourGuide?

Big travel sites list thousands of experiences. RealDays lists fewer on purpose, each run by the people who started it.

How do businesses get listed on RealDays?

Businesses anywhere in Australia can sign up on our page for businesses and list their experiences.
