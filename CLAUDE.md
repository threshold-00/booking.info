# Threshold marketing site

Static marketing site for Threshold, a booking site for small, owner-run experience businesses. The pitch: unique experiences run by the local people who started them, on the Mornington Peninsula and in Bali. Free to join. From 1 May 2027, businesses pay a 12% fee (including GST and card fees) on bookings made through Threshold. No subscription, no setup fee.

Salty Cowboy Bali is the first listed business.

## Deploy

GitHub Pages serves `main` from repo root at **https://thresholdbooking.com/** (custom domain via `CNAME`). Every page carries a `<link rel="canonical">` to the thresholdbooking.com URL, Open Graph and Twitter tags, and a JSON-LD block in `<head>`. Remote is `threshold-00/booking.info`. Every push to `main` redeploys automatically, usually within a minute. Never upload files through the GitHub web UI.

## Local preview

```bash
npx browser-sync start --server --files "index.html, case-study.html, styles.css, app.js" --port 3000 --no-notify --no-ghost-mode
```

`--no-ghost-mode` matters: without it browser-sync mirrors clicks between pages. Preview locally first, push only once the change looks right.

## Conventions (non-negotiable)

- **Never use em-dashes** anywhere: code, copy, prose responses, commit messages. Use a comma or a full stop.
- **Say "businesses", never "operators".** Applies to copy, nav, titles, meta, alt text, JSON-LD, llms.txt, and code comments.
- **No fictional businesses.** Any example card is labelled "Example" in visible text, or removed. The Stripe test-mode demo (Example Woodshop, /demo/b/example-woodshop/) is labelled "Demo account" on its page. It has no homepage card since 21 Oct 2026 (Ro).
- **Business demos** at `/demo/b/<slug>/` (Gordon Studio Glassblowers, Heathcote Bike Hire, Salty Seas Surf School, Tiger Moth World, Spring Creek Horse Rides, Indigo Woodworks, Moonraker Dolphin Swims, Phoenix Pottery, Lygon Street Pottery, simétrie, Suigeneris Atelier, Peachy Keen Ceramics and East Coast Kayaking are published; any others stay local and uncommitted until Ro approves each one) are generated by `node tools/build-business-demos.mjs` from `data/business-layer/<slug>.json` (private). Never edit those pages by hand. They carry the black demo banner (in `booking_mode: sandbox` it names the business and gives the Stripe test card; in `booking_mode: demo` there is no Stripe account and the booking service skips the card step), stay noindex and unlinked (except the thirteen published ones above, which have two homepage cards each with a "Demo" pill, Ro, 21 Oct 2026), and show `[TO CONFIRM]` placeholders until each business confirms. They use the v2 template: gallery header, Demo flag above the heading, Good to know list, booking mode.
- **No empty category pages.** Where a hub has no business yet, use a tasteful "spot waiting to be filled" placeholder (no names, prices, reviews, business schema).
- **No live-availability or real-time calendar claims.** No payment or calendar-sync claims (neither is built).
- **No business pricing or commission on customer pages.** The 12% fee appears only on /for-business/ (and llms.txt).
- **Prefer targeted patches over full-file rewrites.** Edit the specific block, do not regenerate the file.
- **Styling is mostly inline.** Only shared primitives live in `styles.css`. Match the surrounding approach.
- **Design tokens** are the `:root` custom properties in `styles.css`: `--ink` `#17171a`, `--paper` `#f3f2ee`, `--panel` `#f7f6f3`, `--line`, `--display`, `--mono`, and grey hovers `--color-hover` `#e6e4df` (on paper) and `--color-hover-dark` `#333333` (on ink fills). No accent colour: green was retired (Ro, 22 Oct 2026). Links go ink with an underline on hover; success states are ink text plus an icon. Use the variables, do not hardcode new hexes.
- **Type:** body is Chivo Mono, uppercase, 12px. Headings use the Neue Haas Grotesk Display stack, sentence case, weight 500.
- **Responsive:** one breakpoint at `max-width:820px` in `styles.css`. New two-column sections should use `class="split"`.
- **Check all pages** after a change to `styles.css`.

## Site specifics

- **Contact form** posts to Formspree when `FORM_ENDPOINT` at the top of `app.js` is set. Falls back to a mail draft to `CONTACT_EMAIL`. The hidden `_gotcha` input is Formspree's spam trap.
- **Contact address** is `CONTACT_EMAIL` in `app.js`, currently `rowenabaulch@outlook.com`. Hardcoded `mailto:hello@example.com` links are rewritten by `app.js` on load.
- **Experiences section** (`#browse`): Location and Duration dropdowns plus Type chips, combined with AND, and Grid / Map tabs (Grid first). State lives in the URL (`?location=&duration=&cat=&sub=&view=map`); location, duration and view changes add history entries so the back button steps through them. Duration buckets: 60 min or less, 61 to 300, over 300 on one day, two days or more. Each listed experience has `durationMinutes` (and `durationDays` when it spans 2+ days) in its data file (`data/business-layer/<slug>.json` page.experiences, `data/salty-cowboy.json` activities); after changing one or adding a card, run `node tools/card-durations.mjs` to copy them onto the homepage cards (`data-duration`, `data-days`).
- **Map** (`#exploreMap`, the Map tab) uses vendored Leaflet, built the first time the tab opens, and follows the filters: only pins with a matching card, fitted to the chosen location (All locations fits Victoria, or Bali when nothing in Victoria matches). One pin per business location (the `pins` list in `app.js`, suburb-level when an address is private or unconfirmed); each pin shows its matching activities one at a time (photo, title, place, price, arrows and "1 / N"), read from the homepage cards in `#browseGrid`, so new cards appear on the map automatically. A popup on desktop, a bottom sheet at 820px and under. A new business needs one line in `pins`. No placeholder pins.
- **Floating CTA** (`#floatCta`) shows once the hero is off screen.
- **Salty Cowboy** is approved, live and bookable, "From $50 AUD" on card, pin and schema.

## Pricing

Free to join. From Saturday 1 May 2027, businesses pay a 12% fee, including GST, on completed bookings made through Threshold, whatever the booking's source. It's all-in: it covers card and payment fees, with no customer surcharge and no separate card fee. Each business chooses whether to bake it into its price or absorb it. At least 60 days' written notice first (so sent by Tuesday 2 Mar 2027), and businesses can leave without penalty. No subscription, no setup fee, no channel fee. 10% and 8% are retired (Ro, 20 Oct 2026). Keep pricing low-key: one small line, never a headline. The one exception is the "How Threshold compares" table under the pricing line on /for-business/ (Ro, 21 Oct 2026): competitor figures as checked on 21 Oct 2026 with a footnote, and no "cheaper" or "save" claims. This pricing appears only on /for-business/ and in llms.txt, never on customer-facing pages (homepage, hubs, FAQ, case study).

## Default change workflow

1. Make the targeted edit.
2. Preview at http://localhost:3000 and confirm at desktop and under 820px.
3. Show Ro a summary of what changed.
4. If `styles.css`, `app.js` or any shared file in `assets/` (detail.js, demo-booking.js, tokens.css, components.css, booking/) changed, run `node tools/stamp-assets.mjs`. It adds `?v=<hash>` to every reference so browsers fetch the new file instead of a cached one.
5. Commit and push only after Ro approves. Pushing is what publishes the change.
