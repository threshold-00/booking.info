# RealDays marketing site (formerly Threshold)

Static marketing site for RealDays (renamed from Threshold, Ro, 22 Oct 2026), a booking site for small, owner-run experience businesses. The pitch: unique experiences run by the local people who started them, on the Mornington Peninsula and in Bali. Free to join. From 1 May 2027, businesses pay a 12% fee (including GST and card fees) on bookings made through RealDays. No subscription, no setup fee.

User-facing copy says "RealDays" (capital R and D, no space). The domain is getrealdays.com (Ro, 23 Oct 2026); thresholdbooking.com and www 301-redirect to the same path and query on it through a Cloudflare Redirect Rule, so keep that domain renewed. The repo, file and folder names, CSS/JS identifiers and localStorage keys still say threshold; don't rename those without asking Ro.

Salty Cowboy Bali is the first listed business.

## Deploy

GitHub Pages serves `main` from repo root at **https://getrealdays.com/** (custom domain via `CNAME`). Every page carries a `<link rel="canonical">` to the getrealdays.com URL, Open Graph and Twitter tags, and a JSON-LD block in `<head>`. Remote is `threshold-00/booking.info`. Every push to `main` redeploys automatically, usually within a minute. Never upload files through the GitHub web UI.

## Local preview

```bash
npx browser-sync start --server --files "index.html, case-study.html, styles.css, app.js" --port 3000 --no-notify --no-ghost-mode
```

`--no-ghost-mode` matters: without it browser-sync mirrors clicks between pages. Preview locally first, push only once the change looks right.

## Conventions (non-negotiable)

- **Never use em-dashes** anywhere: code, copy, prose responses, commit messages. Use a comma or a full stop.
- **Say "businesses", never "operators".** Applies to copy, nav, titles, meta, alt text, JSON-LD, llms.txt, and code comments.
- **No fictional businesses.** Any example card is labelled "Example" in visible text, or removed. The Stripe test-mode demo (Example Woodshop, /demo/b/example-woodshop/) is labelled "Demo account" on its page. It has no homepage card since 21 Oct 2026 (Ro).
- **Business demos** at `/demo/b/<slug>/` (Gordon Studio Glassblowers, Heathcote Bike Hire, Salty Seas Surf School, Spring Creek Horse Rides, Indigo Woodworks, Moonraker Dolphin Swims, Phoenix Pottery, simétrie, Suigeneris Atelier, Peachy Keen Ceramics, East Coast Kayaking and Aysha Hansen are published (Tiger Moth World was taken down, Ro, 25 Oct 2026; Lygon Street Pottery, Ro, 26 Oct 2026); any others stay local and uncommitted until Ro approves each one) are generated by `node tools/build-business-demos.mjs` from `data/business-layer/<slug>.json` (private). Never edit those pages by hand. They carry the black demo banner (in `booking_mode: sandbox` it names the business and gives the Stripe test card; in `booking_mode: demo` there is no Stripe account and the booking service skips the card step), stay noindex and unlinked (except the twelve published ones above, which have two homepage cards each with a "Demo" pill, Ro, 21 Oct 2026), and show `[TO CONFIRM]` placeholders until each business confirms. They use the v2 template: gallery header, Demo flag above the heading, Good to know list, booking mode.
- **No empty category pages.** Where a hub has no business yet, use a tasteful "spot waiting to be filled" placeholder (no names, prices, reviews, business schema).
- **No live-availability or real-time calendar claims.** No payment or calendar-sync claims (neither is built).
- **No business pricing or commission on customer pages.** The 12% fee appears only on /for-business/ (and llms.txt).
- **Prefer targeted patches over full-file rewrites.** Edit the specific block, do not regenerate the file.
- **Styling is mostly inline.** Only shared primitives live in `styles.css`. Match the surrounding approach.
- **Design tokens** are the `:root` custom properties in `styles.css`: `--ink` `#17171a`, `--paper` `#f3f2ee`, `--panel` `#f7f6f3`, `--line`, `--display`, `--mono`, and grey hovers `--color-hover` `#e6e4df` (on paper) and `--color-hover-dark` `#333333` (on ink fills). No accent colour: green was retired (Ro, 22 Oct 2026). Links go ink with an underline on hover; success states are ink text plus an icon. Use the variables, do not hardcode new hexes.
- **Type:** body is Chivo Mono, uppercase, 12px. Headings use the Neue Haas Grotesk Display stack, sentence case, weight 500.
- **Responsive:** one breakpoint at `max-width:820px` in `styles.css`. New two-column sections should use `class="split"`.
- **RealDays layout (homepage and /for-business/, `body.rd`, Ro's alignment brief, 22 Oct 2026):** one container (`--container` is 1440px less two `--gutter`s; `--gutter` 20/32/48px at <768/768+/1200+, so content runs 48 to 1392 at 1440 and centres above that; Ro may widen the desktop gutter, a one-line change), a 12-column `.grid` with `.c-1-5`, `.c-7-12` etc. from 1024px, the 8px `--s-*` scale, `--t-*` type, and 40px square controls (`.btn--primary`, `.btn--secondary`, `.btn--on-dark`, `.chip`, `.pill-select`; `--r-control` is 0 since Ro's brief 2, 22 Oct 2026; only the card "Demo" tag and the 6px hero search panel are rounded). Cards are one shell (211:158 photo, square on phones, price pinned to the bottom) in `.card-grid` (`--cards-per-row`: 5 from 1200px, 3 from 768px, 2 on phones) or a sideways `.card-rail` whose cards are the same width (`--card-w`). These rules are scoped to `.rd` so other pages keep their own layout until Ro decides to move them over.
- **Check all pages** after a change to `styles.css`.

## Site specifics

- **Contact form** posts to Formspree when `FORM_ENDPOINT` at the top of `app.js` is set. Falls back to a mail draft to `CONTACT_EMAIL`. The hidden `_gotcha` input is Formspree's spam trap.
- **Contact address** is `CONTACT_EMAIL` in `app.js`, currently `rowena@getrealdays.com`. Hardcoded `mailto:hello@example.com` links are rewritten by `app.js` on load.
- **Experiences section** (`#browse`): a filter nav under the hero (What chips, horse sub-chips, Situation chips Group / Duo / Solo with a one-line helper under them, Where, How long, Sort by, View on map) that sticks under the header once scrolled to, as one 56px row of dropdowns (What, Situation, Where, How long, built by app.js) plus Sort by and View on map (phones: Filters, Sort by, map); then two hand-picked rails ("Good for a first go", "Just added") and "All experiences" (`#browseGrid`). Filters combine with AND; with any filter on, the rails hide. The hero search (What, Where, Situation, Go) sets the same filters. State lives in the URL (`?cat=&sub=&who=&location=&duration=&sort=&map=1`); location, duration and map changes add history entries so the back button steps through them, and the old `view=map` opens the map. Each card carries `data-cat` (the What chips: horse, clay, sealife, surf, wood, fashion, bike; Adrenaline went with Tiger Moth World, Ro, 25 Oct 2026), `data-situation` (solo duo group, drafted from group sizes, Ro to confirm) and `data-sort-aud` (price in AUD for Sort by; blank sorts last). Sort options are the `SORT_OPTIONS` list in `app.js`. The Grid / Map tabs are hidden in the markup until Ro decides whether they come back. Duration buckets: 60 min or less, 61 to 300, over 300 on one day, two days or more. Each listed experience has `durationMinutes` (and `durationDays` when it spans 2+ days) in its data file (`data/business-layer/<slug>.json` page.experiences, `data/salty-cowboy.json` activities); after changing one or adding a card, run `node tools/card-durations.mjs` to copy them onto the homepage cards (`data-duration`, `data-days`).
- **Map** (`#exploreMap`, inside the "View on map" panel `#mapPanel`: the right half of the screen from 1024px, from under the header to the bottom, with the filter nav and list reflowing into the left half, over the list with a scrim from 768px, full screen on phones) uses vendored Leaflet, built the first time the panel opens, and follows the filters: only pins with a matching card, fitted to the chosen location (All locations fits Victoria, or Bali when nothing in Victoria matches). One pin per business location (the `pins` list in `app.js`, suburb-level when an address is private or unconfirmed); each pin shows its matching activities one at a time (photo, title, place, price, arrows and "1 / N"), read from the homepage cards in `#browseGrid`, so new cards appear on the map automatically. A popup on desktop, a bottom sheet at 820px and under. A new business needs one line in `pins`. No placeholder pins.
- **Floating CTA** (`#floatCta`) shows once the hero is off screen.
- **Homepage notice** (`#moreSoon`): a full-width cookie-style bar along the bottom ("We're still adding experiences...") that can't be closed (no button, Ro, 26 Oct 2026) and shows on every load. app.js sets `--notice-h` to its height and adds `has-notice` to the body, which pads the page by that much so the footer is never hidden behind it and lifts the floating CTA above it.
- **Map cards** (popup on desktop, floating sheet inside the gutters at 820px and under) carry a "Book now" button to the experience page beside the price.
- **Salty Cowboy** is approved, live and bookable, "From $50 AUD" on card, pin and schema.

## Pricing

Free to join. Until 1 May 2027 businesses only pay Stripe's card fee on each booking (Ro, 25 Oct 2026). From Saturday 1 May 2027, businesses pay a 12% fee, including GST, only on completed bookings made through RealDays (Ro's /for-business/ design, 22 Oct 2026; this replaces "whatever the booking's source"). It's all-in: it covers card and payment fees, with no customer surcharge and no separate card fee. Each business chooses whether to bake it into its price or absorb it. At least 60 days' written notice first (so sent by Tuesday 2 Mar 2027), and businesses can leave without penalty. No subscription, no setup fee, no channel fee. 10% and 8% are retired (Ro, 20 Oct 2026). On /for-business/ the pricing section leads with "Free until May 2027. Then 12% on the bookings we bring you." and one paragraph (brief `briefs/for-business-top-sections-2026-10-25.md`, replacing the "What will the 12% cover?" list); the FAQ "What does it cost?" matches its numbers. Everywhere else pricing stays out. The comparison table sits beside it (Ro, 21 Oct 2026): competitor figures as checked on 21 Oct 2026 with a footnote, and no "cheaper" or "save" claims. This pricing appears only on /for-business/ and in llms.txt, never on customer-facing pages (homepage, hubs, FAQ, case study).

## Default change workflow

1. Make the targeted edit.
2. Preview at http://localhost:3000 and confirm at desktop and under 820px.
3. Show Ro a summary of what changed.
4. If `styles.css`, `app.js` or any shared file in `assets/` (detail.js, demo-booking.js, tokens.css, components.css, booking/) changed, run `node tools/stamp-assets.mjs`. It adds `?v=<hash>` to every reference so browsers fetch the new file instead of a cached one.
5. Commit and push only after Ro approves. Pushing is what publishes the change.
