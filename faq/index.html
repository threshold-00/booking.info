FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What Threshold is, how it's different from a big OTA, and how operators get listed.

What is Threshold?

Threshold is booking software for small, independent tour operators. Each listing asks the questions that matter before a slot is confirmed, so operators stop fielding bookings they have to turn away.

Is Threshold a travel agency or a booking platform?

Threshold is a booking platform, not an agency. You book and pay directly on the operator's page, Threshold doesn't take the booking on the operator's behalf or add its own markup to the price.

How is this different from Viator or GetYourGuide?

Big OTAs optimise for browsing volume across thousands of operators and take a commission on every booking. Threshold is built around screening: every slot carries the conditions (experience level, group size, fitness, whatever the operator actually needs to know) that decide whether a booking should go ahead, answered before the slot confirms rather than after.

Do I need an account to book a tour?

No. You answer the operator's questions, pay, and get your confirmation in one page. There's nothing to set up.

How do operators get listed on Threshold?

Operators set up their own booking page with their own rules (group size caps, time slots, pricing, required screening questions), no code, no developer needed. See the for-operators page for the founding-member offer.

What happens if I don't meet an operator's requirements for a slot?

The booking page tells you before you pay, not after. That's the point of Threshold: operators configure the conditions a slot needs, so a traveller who doesn't meet them finds out immediately instead of after a request sits in an inbox.
