FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What Threshold is, how it's different from a big OTA, and how businesses get listed.

What is Threshold?

Threshold is a booking site for small, owner-run experience businesses. We list unique experiences run by the local people who started them, on the Mornington Peninsula and in Bali.

Is Threshold a travel agency or a booking platform?

Threshold is a booking platform, not an agency. You book directly with the business, and pay the price they set.

How is this different from Viator or GetYourGuide?

Big OTAs list thousands of experiences and take commissions of 20-30%. Threshold lists fewer places on purpose. Every experience is chosen with care, and businesses pay a lower commission so more of your money goes to the people who run the experience.

Do I need an account to book?

No. Pick your date, fill in your details, and book. There's nothing to set up.

How do businesses get listed on Threshold?

Businesses apply through our page for businesses. We choose experiences we'd book ourselves, run by people who care about what they do.

What if I have questions about a booking?

Each business handles their own bookings directly. If you have questions about an experience, you can contact the business through their listing page. For questions about Threshold, use our contact form.
