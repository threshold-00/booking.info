      Overview

      Sui Workshop is the class studio of Suigeneris Atelier, a leather atelier in Mulgrave. Sit at the bench and make your own piece in Italian or French leather, then take it home: a card pouch in two hours, charms and journals in an afternoon, or a tote or bucket bag over a few weeks.

      What's included

        Good to know

          Duration
          Most classes are 2 to 3.5 hours. Passport cover: 2 sessions. Tote: 5 sessions. Bucket bag: 4 to 5. Luxury bag: 8.
          Price
          From $79 (card pouch or coin purse) to $880 (luxury bag). Prices as listed by Sui Workshop.
          Group size
          Up to 8 in most classes, 9 for the bakery charm and 10 for the lucky cat.
          Location
          Mulgrave, Melbourne. You get the studio address when you book.
          Ability
          Most classes are for beginners. The car key cover suits people with some leather experience. The luxury bag is for students who have done four workshops with Sui Workshop.
          Booking
          You book and pay on Sui Workshop's own booking page. Book at least 24 hours ahead.
          Cancellation
          Book at least 24 hours ahead. Bookings can't be cancelled. You can move your class once, with at least 24 hours' notice. No-shows and changes within 24 hours can't be taken. Ask Sui Workshop about any changes through their booking page.
