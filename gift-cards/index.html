How it works

  1. 1Choose a design and an amount, and write the cardSend it to yourself to print or forward, or straight to them on the day you pick.
  2. 2PayIn a live purchase you'd pay by card. This preview skips that step.
  3. 3They choose the experienceAnything booked through RealDays, any time in the next 3 years. They add the code when they book.

Good to know

  • Valid for 3 years from the day it's bought. The date is on the card.
  • Spend it on any experience booked through RealDays, all at once or over several bookings. What you don't spend stays on the card.
  • If the experience costs more than the card, you pay the rest by card when you book.
  • No fees to buy it or use it.
  • Some listings are booked on the business's own website. Those can't take a RealDays gift card.
  • If a business cancels, the amount goes back on the card.
  • Anyone with the code can use it, so keep it safe. A lost or stolen code can't be replaced.
  • Gift cards can't be refunded for a change of mind or swapped for cash. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law still apply, and any refund goes back to the person who bought the card.
