The short version

Five things to know before you read the rest.

  1. 01A customer books and their card is held. You accept or decline within 24 hours, and the card is only charged when you accept.
  2. 02We send your money the day after the experience. Stripe pays it into your bank on its normal schedule.
  3. 03Your first month costs only Stripe's card fee. After that it's 12% of each booking made through RealDays, including GST, and that covers the card fee.
  4. 04Your own cancellation policy applies when a customer cancels. If you cancel, the customer gets a full refund and you cover that booking's card fee.
  5. 05No lock-in. Either of us can end the agreement at any time.

This summary is here to help. The terms below are what you agree to.

The terms

RealDays business terms

These terms are an agreement between you, the business listed on RealDays, and Rowena Louise Baulch, trading as RealDays (ABN 90 522 324 404), email rowena@getrealdays.com ("RealDays", "we", "us").

They apply from the day you sign them below.

1What RealDays does

1.1RealDays lists your experiences on getrealdays.com, takes booking requests and payments from customers, and sends you your share.

1.2You run the experience. The contract for the experience is between you and the customer. RealDays isn't a party to it and doesn't run, supervise or guarantee it.

1.3There's no lock-in and no exclusivity. You can list your experiences elsewhere too.

2Your listing

2.1You give us the details for your page: what the experience is, prices, times, group sizes, what's included, location, age or fitness requirements, and your cancellation policy. You confirm the page is accurate before it goes live, and tell us when anything changes.

2.2We may edit your wording for length, layout and clarity, but we won't change prices, times or your cancellation policy without asking you.

2.3You own your photos, logo and words, or have permission to use them. You let RealDays use them on your page and to promote your listing on RealDays (for example on our homepage, social media and ads) while you're listed, and you can ask us to remove any of them.

2.4You set your own prices. They are the prices customers pay. RealDays adds no booking fee or surcharge for the customer.

3Bookings

3.1A customer books on your page and pays by card through Stripe. The card is held, not charged, and the place is held for them.

3.2We email you each request with Accept and Decline buttons. If you accept, the card is charged and the booking is confirmed to you both. If you decline, or don't answer within [24] hours, the hold is released and the customer isn't charged.

3.3You only accept a booking you can honour, and keep your availability on RealDays up to date so customers can't book times you can't run.

3.4We share the customer's name, email, mobile and booking answers with you so you can run the booking.

4Payments

4.1RealDays collects payment as your agent. When a customer pays RealDays for your booking, that counts as the customer paying you. If we fail to send you your share, that's a matter between you and RealDays, not the customer.

4.2Payments are processed by Stripe. To receive them you set up a Stripe Express account from the link we send and accept Stripe's Connected Account Agreement (stripe.com/au/legal/connect-account), which forms part of these terms. Your business name appears on the customer's card statement.

4.3When you're paid. We send your share to your Stripe account at about 6am Melbourne time on the day after the experience. Stripe then pays it into your bank on its normal schedule, usually within a few business days. For a course of several weekly classes, we send it the day after the first class.

4.4We take card bookings up to 80 days before the experience. We hold the customer's money until we send your share, so a refund before the experience never comes out of your bank account.

4.5If your Stripe account can't receive money (for example Stripe needs more details from you), we hold your share, tell you, and send it once Stripe allows.

5Fees

5.1First month: from the day your listing goes live, for one month, you pay only Stripe's card fee on each booking (currently about 1.7% + A$0.30 on an Australian card, and more on overseas cards). It's taken off what we send you.

5.2After that: a fee of 12% of the booking price, including GST, on bookings made through RealDays. It covers the card fee, so you don't pay both. It's taken off what we send you. For example, on a A$120 booking we send you A$105.60.

5.3There's no subscription, setup fee or listing fee.

5.4The 12% is worked out on the full booking price, before any discount code or gift card.

5.5If we've agreed different fees with you in writing, those apply instead of 5.1 and 5.2.

5.6RealDays is registered for GST. Ask us any time for a tax invoice for our fees, and we'll send it within 28 days. You're responsible for the GST and tax on your own sales.

5.7We'll give you at least 60 days' written notice of any change to our fees. You can leave without penalty before it starts.

6Cancellations and refunds

6.1When a customer cancels, your cancellation policy, as shown on your page when they booked, decides any refund. An admin or cancellation fee only applies if it was in the policy the customer saw. We process the refund through Stripe.

6.2When you cancel a booking, for any reason including weather or not enough people, the customer is offered another date or a full refund. If they're refunded, Stripe's card fee on that booking comes off your next payout.

6.3If you cancel one class of a multi-week course, you offer a make-up class or refund that class.

6.4Nothing in these terms limits a customer's rights under the Australian Consumer Law, and nothing in your cancellation policy can either.

7Disputes and chargebacks

7.1If a customer disputes a payment for a booking with you, RealDays answers the dispute with Stripe. Please send us anything that shows the booking went ahead.

7.2RealDays' Stripe account is charged first. If the bank decides in the customer's favour, for any reason, including fraud, you carry the cost: the disputed amount isn't paid to you, or, if it has already been paid to you, it's taken back from your Stripe account. Stripe's dispute fee (currently A$25) is also yours, and comes off your next payout.

7.3Anything you owe RealDays (a card fee, a dispute fee, or money we couldn't take back) comes off your next payouts until it's repaid. If no payouts are due within 30 days, you agree to pay it to us within 14 days of our invoice. RealDays may choose to cover a case.

8.1You run each experience as described on your page, safely, and in line with any law, licence or permit that applies to it.

8.2You hold public liability insurance of at least A$10 million that covers the experiences you list, and show us a certificate if we ask.

8.3You're responsible for your customers' safety during the experience, and for any waiver, safety briefing or age or health check it needs.

8.4You use customers' details only to run their booking. You don't add them to marketing lists unless they agree to it themselves, and you keep their details secure.

8.5You deal with customers fairly, and you comply with the Australian Consumer Law.
