Our why

How did RealDays begin?

RealDays started in 2026 with a simple aim: help small, owner-run experience businesses get back to doing the work they love.

Our founder, Ro, kept meeting owners stuck in a loop of customer enquiries and admin, with less time left for their craft. So she visited a range of adventure and creator-based businesses to find out what would actually help.

She found they wanted to get both customers in the door and be able to manage enquiries without being across many different channels.

Frequently asked questions

What is RealDays?

A new marketplace where people in Victoria find and book local experiences from small, owner-run businesses: adventures, creative workshops, and food and drink classes. Businesses anywhere in Australia can join.

What does it cost?

Nothing to list until 1 January 2027. Until then, bookings made through RealDays carry only Stripe’s card fee. From 1 January 2027 it’s 15% of each booking we bring you, including GST. It covers the card fee, so you don’t pay both. There’s nothing on bookings you take yourself, no monthly fee, no setup fee and no lock-in. It’s all in your terms.

Do I keep my own booking tool?

Yes. Keep your own booking tool and website, and keep taking bookings there. RealDays is more people finding you. When someone books on RealDays, they pay on RealDays and we email you their details straight away.

How do I get on RealDays?

Fill in the form at the top of this page and add a class you’re running in the next 7 days (paste a link to its page and we fill in most of it). Then confirm your email and set up payouts with Stripe. Your class goes live once both are done. Or email Ro a link to your experience at rowena@getrealdays.com and we’ll set up your listing for you to check.

How will people find me?

On getrealdays.com and in our weekly email to people who asked for it, and we feature new businesses on our Instagram and in our ads. We’re new, so we can’t promise how many bookings that brings.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay by card on RealDays when they book. We send your share to your own Stripe account the day after the class, and Stripe pays it into your bank, usually within a few business days.

Do I have to accept each booking?

No. A booking for a class you add here is confirmed as soon as the customer pays, so only add places you can take. Bookings close 2 hours before the start. You get the customer’s name, email and mobile.

What if someone cancels?

Bookings for a class you add here are final for customers, so there’s no refund if they cancel. If you have to cancel, the customer gets a full refund.

What are last-minute spots?

An extra you can use if it suits you: a quiet class’s spare places, up to 7 days ahead, at a last-minute price you set. It can be your normal price. You can also add up to two lower prices that start closer to the class. Customers see the price as, for example, $130 per person, usually $170.
