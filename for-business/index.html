RealDays started in 2026 with a simple aim: help small, owner-run experience businesses get back to doing the work they love.

Our founder, Ro, kept meeting owners stuck in a loop of customer enquiries and admin, with less time left for their craft. So she visited a range of adventure and creator-based businesses to find out what would actually help.

She found they wanted to get both customers in the door and be able to manage enquiries without being across many different channels.

Frequently asked questions

What is RealDays?

A booking site for small, owner-run class and experience businesses. You post spare places in a class that isn’t full, at a last-minute price, and we put them in front of customers who want something to do this week. Businesses anywhere in Australia can join.

How do I post a last-minute spot?

Fill in the form at the top of this page and add your class straight away (paste a link to its page and we fill in most of it). Then confirm your email and set up payouts with Stripe. Your spot goes live once both are done.

What does it cost?

Free to join, with no monthly fee and no setup fee. You only pay when a spot sells: our fee comes out of that booking and covers the card fees, so there’s nothing to pay up front and nothing if a place doesn’t sell. The fees are in your terms.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay by card on RealDays when they book. The money goes to your own Stripe account, less our fee.

Which classes should I post?

Any class in the next 7 days that has places left and looks unlikely to fill. We suggest posting about a week out, so there’s time to sell them. You choose how many places to release.

Can I set the price?

Yes. You set your normal price and the last-minute price, and you can add up to two drops that kick in closer to the start. Customers see the price as, for example, $130 per person, usually $170. The price is locked when they pay.

Do I have to accept each booking?

No. A spot booking is confirmed as soon as the customer pays, so only release places you can take. Bookings close 2 hours before the start. You get the customer’s name, email and mobile.

What if someone cancels?

Last-minute spots are final for customers, so there’s no refund if they cancel. If you have to cancel, the customer gets a full refund.
