Good to know

Getting there

These experiences are spread across Victoria, from Melbourne to Geelong, the Mornington Peninsula and the Indigo Valley. Each listing says where it is. A car makes the regional ones easier to reach.

When to visit

Summer (December to February) is busiest, with warm weather and longer days. Autumn and spring are quieter, and many experiences run year-round.
