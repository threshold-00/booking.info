Good to know

Getting there

This part of Victoria, Australia is about an hour's drive south of Melbourne. Public transport runs to some towns, but having a car makes it easier to explore.

When to visit

Summer (December to February) is busiest, with warm weather and longer days. Autumn and spring are quieter, and many experiences run year-round.
