Booking terms

Updated 8 November 2026

These terms apply when you book an experience on RealDays (getrealdays.com). RealDays is run by Rowena Baulch, trading as RealDays (ABN 90 522 324 404). Each experience is run by the local business that offers it, and RealDays takes the booking and payment for them.

Who you're booking with

  • Your booking is with the business that runs the experience. They're responsible for running it as described on their page, safely, and for anything that happens during it.
  • RealDays takes your booking and your payment as the business's agent. When you pay RealDays for a booking, you've paid the business.
  • RealDays is responsible for the booking service itself: taking your booking, sending it to the business, taking payment, sending your emails and processing any refund.

How booking works

  • You choose a date, time and number of people, add your details, and add your card through Stripe. Your card is held, not charged.
  • The business confirms your booking within the time shown when you book, usually 24 hours. When they confirm, your card is charged and we email you the details.
  • If they can't confirm in time, or the place is no longer free, the hold on your card is released and you aren't charged.
  • For some experiences you ask for a day, and the business confirms the time with you.
  • If an experience takes a deposit, the page shows how much it is and when the rest is due, and we email you a link to pay the rest.

Prices and payment

  • The price shown is the price you pay, in Australian dollars, including GST where it applies. RealDays adds no booking fee or card surcharge.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • The business's name appears on your card statement.

If you need to cancel

  • Use the cancel link in your booking emails. It shows what you'll get back before you confirm.
  • The business's cancellation policy, as shown on their page when you booked, decides any refund. A fee only applies if it was in the policy you saw.
  • If your booking hasn't been confirmed yet, cancelling releases the hold and you aren't charged.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show them.
  • If you'd rather move to another date, reply to your booking email and we'll ask the business. It's up to them whether they can move you.

If the business cancels

If the business cancels, for any reason including weather or not enough people, you're offered another date or a full refund. If one class of a multi-week course is cancelled, you're offered a make-up class or a refund for that class.

Before and on the day

  • Read the experience's page and booking emails for what to bring, age or fitness requirements, and where to go.
  • Answer the business's booking questions honestly, such as your experience level, so they can run it safely.
  • Follow the business's safety instructions. They may ask you to sign a waiver or do a safety briefing first.
  • If you arrive late or don't come, the cancellation policy applies as if you'd cancelled at the start time.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled to cancel your service contract and to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced value. You are also entitled to be compensated for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. If the failure does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have problems with the service rectified in a reasonable time and, if this is not done, to cancel your contract and obtain a refund for the unused portion of the contract.

Nothing in these terms, or in a business's cancellation policy, limits those rights.

If something goes wrong

  • For anything about the experience itself, talk to the business first. They're best placed to put it right.
  • For anything about your booking or payment, or if you can't sort it out with the business, reply to your booking email or email rowena@getrealdays.com and we'll help.
  • Please talk to us before disputing a payment with your bank. It's usually quicker for you.

Your information

We share your name, email, mobile and booking details with the business so they can run your booking. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Changes and law

We may update these terms. The terms on this page when you booked apply to that booking. They're governed by the law of Victoria, Australia.
