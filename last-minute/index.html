Why join

Real classes, spare places, a lower price.

How the emails work

One email, once a week.

  1. Join with your email

    That's all we ask for. You're on the list straight away.

  2. Thursdays at midday

    We send the week's spare places, grouped by day, in time to plan your weekend. If there are no spots that week, there's no email.

  3. Everything on one line

    Each spot shows the class, the business, the time, how many places are left, and the price next to the usual price.

  4. Book this spot

    Tap it to see the details and book. The spot's page always shows what's left.

  5. Leave any time

    Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
