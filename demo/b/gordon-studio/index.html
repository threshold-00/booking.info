        Overview

        Spend a day in the hot shop with a family of glass artists working across three generations on the Mornington Peninsula. In the hands-on workshop you make two paperweights and a tumbler, ready to take home the following week. Or come for a Friday night and watch the glassblowers work, with Fenian wines and cheese.

          Good to know

            Duration
            Workshop runs 9:30am to 4pm (arrive 9:15am). Friday Night with the Glassblowers runs 5:30pm to 8:30pm.
            Price
            Workshop $500 per person. Friday night from $70. The gallery and viewing balcony are free to visit.
            Location
            290 Red Hill Road, Red Hill VIC 3937. Gallery open Thu to Mon, 10am to 4pm, closed Tue and Wed.
            Age or ability
            No experience needed. Workshops take up to 4 people. Minimum age for the workshop: 16 years.
            What to bring
            Your own lunch. Wear loose clothes and closed shoes, and tie long hair back.
            Cancellation (workshop)
            Cancel 2 weeks or more before the start for a 50% refund. Less than 2 weeks before, no refund unless the business cancels or reschedules. If minimum numbers aren't met 48 hours out, the workshop is rescheduled. [TO CONFIRM: Friday night event policy]
