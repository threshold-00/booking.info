        Overview

        Adventure Flight Co flies high-G aerobatics in ex-military Nanchang CJ-6A warbirds from Moorabbin and Barwon Heads, a WWII-era fighter trainer that goes from 0 to 300km/h in under 5 seconds. It's just you and an experienced pilot, who starts gently and builds up the G force and difficulty as you go, talking you through every manoeuvre. Nervous flyer or adrenaline seeker, it can be mild or wild, it's up to you.

            Over 6,000 warbird flights flown. The planes are maintained to the standards that apply to civilian commercial aviation, and every pilot is very experienced before they fly them.
            Before you book
            This is an adventure flight, not a charter or scenic flight. The plane is an ex-military type without normal civilian certification. It isn't required to comply with the safety regulations for standard aircraft, its design, manufacture and airworthiness aren't required to meet any safety standard recognised by CASA, and there is risk in flying it. You fly at your own risk. The business flies you in VH-ALO or VH-NNM only once you understand and accept this.

              Adventure Flight Co

              Adventure Flight Co began in 2009 as Warbird Air Adventures, flying aerobatic joy flights in the Nanchang CJ-6A, a trainer built for air forces. Its pilots are airline, aerobatic and RAAF pilots with thousands of hours of aerobatics between them, and it has flown over 6,000 warbird flights.
