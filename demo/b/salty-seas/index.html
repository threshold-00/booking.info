        Overview

        Surf lessons on the Bass Coast with head coach Allison and senior coach Lily, run as a family business with a "surfing is better together" feel. Choose a 90-minute private lesson, a four-day kids' program, or the women's surf groups, which are grouped by ability and timed to the tides. Sessions move with the tides, wind and swell, so every one is planned around the day's conditions.
