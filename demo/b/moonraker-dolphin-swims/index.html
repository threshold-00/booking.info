        Overview

        A family business that's been taking people out on Port Phillip Bay for 36 years. Slip into the water beside wild dolphins, fur seals and weedy sea dragons, with all your gear, a warm drink and lunch looked after. Not a swimmer? The wildlife cruise stays on board.
